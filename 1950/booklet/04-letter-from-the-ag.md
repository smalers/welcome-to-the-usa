# A Welcome to USA "Citizenship" / Letter from the Attorney General #

(Letter with official letterhead)

***Office of the Attorney General***

***Washington, D.C.***
  
  
  
  
Dear Fellow Citizen:

It is my happy privilege to share in the momentous
occassion of your becoming a citizen of the United States, not
only through my official representative but also this personal
word of welcome.

The Department of Justice, thorugh the Immigration
and Naturalization Service, is charged with assisting persons
seeking naturalization.  It has, therefore, been the pleasant
duty of members of our official family to work with you during
the period of your preparations for this eventful day in your life.

We are glad to have been able to help you achieve your
goal, and are happy to welcome you sincerely and cordially
into the full privileges and responsibilities of American
citizenship.

J. Howard McGrath

Attorney General
